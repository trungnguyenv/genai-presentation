# Generative AI Talk

A talk for my team of developers and testers, meant to get them adopting and excited about generative AI.

## Language

**Talk**:
The whole session given to the team: the slides plus the live demo, delivered once on a screen-shared call.
_Avoid_: Presentation, session, deck (when meaning the whole thing)

**Slides**:
The slide deck shown during the talk, excluding the demo.
_Avoid_: Deck, presentation

**Demo**:
The part of the talk where the presenter drives a real generative AI tool live in front of the team.
_Avoid_: Walkthrough, showcase

**Talk spec**:
The decided plan for the talk (outline, the point of each slide, and the demo script), ready to hand off for building.
_Avoid_: Plan, outline (when meaning the whole spec)

**Audience**:
The team the talk is given to: developers and testers with limited exposure to generative AI.
_Avoid_: Attendees, users

**Copilot Chat group**:
The part of the audience whose only AI tool is Microsoft Copilot Chat.
_Avoid_: Chat users, basic group

**IDE group**:
The part of the audience who have Copilot Chat plus an AI coding tool in their IDE, either Cursor or Claude.
_Avoid_: Agent group, advanced group, power users

## Ideas the talk teaches

**Grilling**:
Having the AI interview you, one round of questions at a time, until you and it share an understanding of the task, instead of writing a long prompt yourself.
_Avoid_: Prompt engineering (when meaning this), interviewing

**Skill**:
A reusable prompt that sets how the AI works on a kind of task, such as grilling. An agent tool loads it on demand; in a chat tool you paste it in at the start of a new chat.
_Avoid_: Template, system prompt, custom instructions

**Environment inspection**:
Letting the AI look at the real material (files, command output, the actual document or error) instead of working from your description of it.
_Avoid_: Context gathering, giving context

**Prototype**:
A cheap, rough version of the output that you make so you can react to something concrete before deciding.
_Avoid_: Draft, mockup, POC
